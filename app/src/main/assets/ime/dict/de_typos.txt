# Same format and rules as en_typos.txt (see there): read by TypoCatalog, matched lowercased, the typed
# capitalisation carried over onto the correction, which keeps its own noun capital otherwise
# (standart -> Standard). Grammar confusions between real words (das/dass, seid/seit, tod/tot) are not
# typos and are deliberately absent, as are spellings the 1996/2006 reforms left as accepted variants.

# them out — but generate.py drops them explicitly as well, so that stays true.
